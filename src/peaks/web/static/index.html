Upgrades

Keepers whose copy isn't good enough — picture below Good, an upscale, or under 4K. Peaks searches Prowlarr for healthy releases that are this scene and beat your copy; nothing downloads until you press Grab. The new copy comes in through your seedbox pull, and Duplicates lets you compare and keep the better one.

Duplicates

Recommended = the best picture by your learned bar once every copy is measured (scores within 5 are a tie), else most pixels (8K VR beats 4K) → bitrate → size. ▶ plays a copy; ⧉ Compare copies plays them together at the same moment. "Keep this" queues at once — deletions run in the background; the kept copy takes the group's highest tier first.

Peaks looks at a few full-resolution stills of each file — real detail (upscales read as what they came from), compression blocks, grain, banding, interlacing, letterboxing — and learns which pictures you find good enough from your picks in Taste → Picture. Upgrades (Prowlarr)

          
Peaks searches your Prowlarr indexers for better copies of keepers that need one, and keeps only healthy torrents of the same scene that beat your copy. It never downloads on its own: Grab fetches that one release through Prowlarr and adds it to your seedbox qBittorrent, in the category below, so your pull brings it home. Apart from that add (and the purge's deletes), Peaks only reads qBittorrent.

          
ProwlarrURL and API key (Prowlarr → Settings → General)
